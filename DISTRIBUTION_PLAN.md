## 4. Value-First Community Participation Rule

When engaging in curing communities:
1. **Never post promotional links.**
2. **Prioritize food safety and regulatory accuracy.**
3. **Always give the full calculation directly in the post:**
   > *"If you are curing a 2.5 kg pork loin for lonza using equilibrium curing, you should target 2.5% total salt and exactly 156.25 PPM nitrite for safe preservation against C. botulinum.
   > - Prague Powder #1 required: 2,500g × 0.0025 = 6.25g.
   > - Because Prague Powder is 93.75% common salt, those 6.25g contribute 5.86g of salt.
   > - To reach your target 2.5% salt (62.5g total), you only add 56.64g of pure non-iodized salt (62.5g - 5.86g). If you don't subtract this, your meat will be too salty.
   > - For hanging: target a 35% to 40% weight loss (finished weight: 1,500g–1,625g) at 52–55°F and 75–80% RH.
   > If you want to double-check the exact water activity and thickness penetration days, CureCraft is an open-source USDA math calculator: https://mohd34.github.io/curecraft/"*

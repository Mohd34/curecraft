Terms & Food Safety Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

1. Food Safety & Biological Risks

The curing, fermenting, and dry-aging of meat involves biological processes that, if conducted improperly, carry serious risks of foodborne illness, including but not limited to Clostridium botulinum, Listeria monocytogenes, Salmonella, and Staphylococcus aureus.

2. Educational and Calculation Purpose

CureCraft is an educational and mathematical aid designed to assist users in calculating proportions based on recognized guidelines (such as the USDA FSIS Processing Guidelines). You are solely responsible for:

  • Verifying the accuracy of your digital scales (using 0.01g resolution for curing salts).
  • Confirming the exact chemical formulation on your specific curing salt package (e.g. 6.25% NaNO₂ vs 0.60% NaNO₂).
  • Maintaining sanitary preparation environments and calibrated temperature/humidity controls.
  • Inspecting meats for spoilage, off-odors, or discoloration before consumption.

3. Limitation of Liability

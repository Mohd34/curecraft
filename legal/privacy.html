Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Architecture

CureCraft is an entirely client-side web utility. All calculations (meat weights, salt ratios, curing times, and moisture losses) are performed locally inside your device's web browser using native JavaScript.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information, recipe formulas, or IP addresses. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or behavioral advertising networks.

3. Local Storage

If you choose to save custom recipes or presets, this data is saved exclusively inside your browser's private `localStorage` and never leaves your machine.

How Biltong Achieves Food Safety Without Nitrites

Unlike European salumi or American bacon, traditional South African biltong uses no curing salts or chemical nitrites.

Safety is achieved through three synergistic hurdles:

  1. Acetic Acid Barrier: Dipping the beef strips in brown vinegar or Worcestershire sauce lowers surface pH below 4.6, immediately inhibiting bacterial growth.
  2. Rapid Convective Drying: Slices are dried in a warm, gentle air-stream (a biltong box with a 40W bulb or low-speed fan at 68°F–77°F / 20°C–25°C), quickly dehydrating the exterior before mold can germinate.
  3. High Coarse Coriander Load: Coriander seed contains natural essential oils (primarily linalool) that possess proven antimicrobial properties against foodborne pathogens.

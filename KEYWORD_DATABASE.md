## 2. Dynamic Discovery Protocol

As Google Search Console begins aggregating query impressions, this keyword portfolio will be refreshed weekly. Unanticipated long-tail variations discovered in GSC will be ingested directly into `data/keywords.json` and prioritized via the Opportunity Score Formula:
$$\text{Opportunity Score} = \text{Impressions} \times \text{Relevance} \times \left(\frac{1}{\text{Position}}\right) \times (\text{Benchmark CTR} - \text{Actual CTR})$$

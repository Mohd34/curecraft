## 2. Curing Chamber Environmental Benchmarks

- **Target Temperature:** 50°F – 55°F (10°C – 13°C)  
  *(Colder slows drying; warmer risks pathogen proliferation)*
- **Target Relative Humidity (RH):** 75% – 80%  
  *(Below 70% risks case hardening; above 85% causes excessive wild mold)*
- **Airflow:** Gentle indirect circulation (computer fan cycling 15 min every 2 hours)

## 4. Safety & Quality Evaluation Checklist

- [ ] **Case Hardening Assessment:** Squeeze test shows uniform firmness throughout. No soft squishy center encased in an impenetrable dry outer crust.
- [ ] **Surface Mold Inspection:**
  - [ ] Chalky white / ivory mold present (*Penicillium nalgiovense* — beneficial).
  - [ ] NO black (*Aspergillus niger*), hairy grey (*Mucor*), or slime molds present.
  - *(Action if undesirable mold appears: wipe with 50/50 apple cider vinegar and water solution immediately).*
- [ ] **Water Activity Threshold:** Projected $a_w \le 0.88$ (Achieved at $\ge 35\%$ moisture loss with $2.5\%$ initial salt).
